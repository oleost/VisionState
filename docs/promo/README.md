# Promo pictures

The screenshots in the [README](../../README.md), and `collage.png`: all of them on one picture
(2048 px wide) to share on social media, made by `scripts/promo_collage.py`. Run it again after
the screenshots are taken again (`CLAUDE.md`, *Docs screenshots*).

The collage, from the top:

1. `logo-dark.png` and the README's tagline.
2. `objects.png` (the Driveway object sensor's Live tab: Tom by his face, Our car by its plate)
   beside `ha-objects.png` (that sensor as a device in Home Assistant).
3. `dashboard.png` (the Sensors page: Driveway, Garage door, Hallway) beside `ha-states.png`
   (the Garage door state sensor as a device in Home Assistant).
4. `people.png` (the People page with Tom's faces), `reading.png` (the Water meter reading
   sensor) and `sound.png` (the Hallway sound sensor hearing a smoke alarm).

Everything in them is demo data (`scripts/seed_demo.py`) on the test photos, which are CC0 or public
domain ([`tests/assets/README.md`](../../visionstate/backend/tests/assets/README.md)).
